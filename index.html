The part I should have done on time

A real sorry.

I missed wishing you on the 22nd. I know that day mattered, and I know I should have been the person wishing you first.

For Pigguuu · from Riggu

I am genuinely sorry.

Pigguuu, I know the 22nd mattered. I know I should have wished you on time, and I don't want to hide behind an excuse or pretend it was a small thing just because I am writing this now.

You have made time for me even when you were busy, even when exams were stressing you, even when things were not easy. So missing our 9-month date is something I genuinely regret.

This website is not a replacement for the moment I missed. It is just my honest attempt to say what I should have said that day: happy 9 months, my Pigguuu.

Thank you for every little bit of love that does not make noise. Thank you for the calls, the care, the stupid moments, the soft moments, the arguments, the hugs, the waiting, and all the times you have simply been there.

Late by two days, but not late in loving you.

— Riggu ♥
